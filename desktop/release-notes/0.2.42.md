# WING CONTROLLER Desktop v0.2.42

Build: 20260922.15 (macOS Apple Silicon)
Date: 2026-09-22

What changes since 0.2.41: the pixel aesthetics where modern glow was left, and the meter banks in both
windows just as the operator wanted them.

## Aesthetics: no glows or smooth gradients

- **ENABLED (macro list)**: the fill is the **pixel dither pattern** from the dither module (the green
  preset, which existed unused), with a 1 px bevel facing out. Before it was a smooth gradient with a
  halo, which clashes in a classic window; now it reads as a button of the era and you can still see at a
  glance which one is active.
- **ACTIVE (OSC monitor)**: out go the **blinking** (`oscPulse`) and the halo. Flat colours and a 1 px
  bevel: the state is readable without anything glowing.
- **"WING CONTROLLER" in the header**: the name carried a green halo in the dark theme and the BETA badge
  a yellow one; both are gone, plain text. The logo no longer lights up on hover (the button background
  changes, like a classic button) and the pressed state is kept.
- **Two other glows gone**: the insertion line when dragging a card (amber bar with an 8 px halo) is now a
  flat line with hard 1 px edges, and the little square of the colour tag no longer glows with its own
  colour (pixel bevel).
- The peak and the CLIP lamp inside the meters **stay on purpose** (there the light is the instrument and
  it warns of clipping) and so do the HUD alert flashes (they respond to the *Flash* setting).

## Meter banks: the same groups in both windows

What the operator wanted: **to reuse in the dock the groups he makes in the popup**, with each window
showing its own.

- **The dock chooses banks**: the Live Meters card selector lists **the same banks** as the Meter Bridge
  (`wing_meter_banks`) and shows only that group.
- **Independent and remembered selection**: the dock keeps its own in `wing_dock_bank` and the bridge its
  own in `wing_meters_bank`, so changing bank in one window does **not** change what the other shows, and
  each recovers its own when it is opened again.
- **The bank rules, even if it is wider than the watchlist**: if a group has meters that are not in that
  window's watchlist, they are shown anyway and **subscribed** (the stream only brings what is subscribed,
  so without that the group would come out empty), and the row says so: *"2 of its 2 meters are not in this
  watchlist and are being listened to anyway"*. Going back to *All meters* releases them.
- **Creating, renaming, updating and deleting** stays in the Meter Bridge, which asks which meters go into
  each bank (a bank is a subset, not a copy of the watchlist).
- The bank row is a **view** control, not a settings one: the *Only meters* mode does not hide it, so you
  can change group without leaving the mode.
