# WING CONTROLLER Desktop v0.2.43

Build: 20260922.16 (macOS Apple Silicon)
Date: 2026-09-22

Two things reported by the operator, both measured in the real window before touching them.

## The right column is no longer cut off when opening

When opening the app, the right column appeared **clipped** by the window edge, and it fixed itself on
connecting. Measured: between **~1041 px and ~1189 px** wide, the layout did not fit (480 px of the builder
+ the seam + the column) and **the page overflowed horizontally**; at 1041 px there were **83 px** to spare
because the header's `.master-stats` also measures 444 px and pushed *Properties…* and *? Help* out of the
window. On connecting the labels change, the content shrinks and that is why it "fixed itself".

- The **right column is limited to the free space** that is left.
- Below **1180 px** the window goes to **a single column** (before the cut-off was at 1040).
- In that band the **master header stacks**, as it already did further down.
- **"Disconnected" becomes "Offline"** in the status chip, in the card summary and in the header button: it
  was the widest label and the one that pushed the buttons out.

Verified with the real layout from **980 to 1440 px**: zero horizontal overflow and no control outside the
window.

## The light theme is classic grey, not a black panel

In the light theme there were four almost black surfaces that stood out too much in a grey window. They
are now **sunken grey panels with classic ink**, like a text box of the era:

- **OSC terminal**: grey, with **navy blue** for what arrives from the console (RX) and **crimson** for
  what is sent (TX).
- **Readouts** (session log, stream and probe) and the **macro summary** (WHEN / IF / THEN): grey with dark
  text.
- **Macro list**: from white to face grey, with the **selection in navy blue**.
- **Meters**: grey card and number in dark ink, but **the bar's recess stays dark**: that is what keeps the
  green/amber LED legible. It stays like an instrument embedded in the panel.

The **dark theme does not change**: it keeps the CRT surfaces and the terminal green.
