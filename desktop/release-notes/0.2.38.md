# WING CONTROLLER Desktop v0.2.38

Build: 20260922.11 (macOS Apple Silicon)
Date: 2026-09-22

It fixes a regression seen live: the side column meters could be left **without a signal**, and the
selection and *update* of custom banks were broken.

## The side column never runs out of meters

The bank filter returned an **empty list** when none of the bank's meters was in the current watchlist (a
bank saved with other channels, or a reloaded watchlist). Result: no meter was painted and there was no
explanation on screen.

Now, if the active bank gives **no** match at all, **all** the meters are shown and the bank is released:
the selector goes back to *"All meters"*, which is what is really being displayed. A bank can never leave
the column empty.

Checked with data: three meters in the watchlist and a bank with unrelated meters → **3 visible** and the
selector on *All meters*.

## Update bank works again (and is understandable)

- **It did not repaint.** The selector fix covered the bank change, but not this button: it updated the
  bank keys and the list stayed as it was. Now it repaints.
  Checked: a bank of 1 key with 3 meters in the watchlist → after updating, **3 keys and 3 visible**.
- **It is easier to understand**: the button is called **"Update bank"** and the dialog says exactly what
  it does — it replaces the contents and **the name is kept** — with the confirm button as *"Replace
  contents"*.
- **Saving a new bank also repaints**, because the newly created bank becomes active and filters the list.

## What already came from 0.2.37

The meter style independent per window (the popup and the side column no longer drag each other) and the
bank selector really filtering.
