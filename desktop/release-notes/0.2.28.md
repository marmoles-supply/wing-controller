# WING CONTROLLER Desktop v0.2.28

Build: 20260922.1 (macOS Apple Silicon)
Date: 2026-09-22

This is the first release that gathers **all the work of the UI/UX re-audit** of 2026-09-21.
The full report — what was fixed, what was dismissed as a false positive and what is still open — is in
`docs/UI_UX_REAUDIT_2026-09-21.md`.

## What it brings over 0.2.27

### Click, close and dialogs (0.2.25–0.2.27, already released, consolidated here)

- **Typing after a click works**: WebKit keeps the DOM focus but closes its editing *pipeline*;
  `placeCaret()` re-applies the selection after focusing. Before, it only worked by holding the
  mouse button down.
- **Closing the app closes it**: `core:window:allow-destroy` was missing, and macOS keeps the process
  after the last window closes.
- **Quitting always asks** (close button and Cmd+Q, with its own menu).
- Compact confirmation dialogs (400 px), a click selects the value, settings legends that
  cut their line, and the column seam from 24 px to 6 px.

### Validation, banks and state (0.2.27)

- **Field validation**: numbers with a range/unit, channel ids with grammar and family size,
  OSC address, on/off and choice parameters. What is invalid is marked **where it is** and saving or
  arming is refused with the reason. An empty field is a legitimate draft.
- **Custom banks in the Meter Bridge** (create from the watchlist, rename, refresh, delete) and
  now **also in the workstation window**, sharing `wing_meter_banks`.

### New in this release

- **Trigger latency metrics** in Diagnostics: from the arrival of the datagram to the moment the
  action is emitted to the window, with p50/p95 and the worst case over the last 256 samples. The real
  scale of the meters (`1/256 dB → dBFS`, Maillot's native protocol) is stated on the same card.
- **Honest connection state**: a new `failed` state ("Cannot reach console") distinct from
  `offline` ("Connection lost") and `idle` ("Disconnected"), with the error visible under Connect + Listen.
- **Live Safe Mode visible**: `[LOCKED]` chip in the header with the reason, guards on the courtesy
  notice, the colour label and reordering, arming that refuses too while `connecting`/
  `reconnecting`, and **in-memory rollback** if a library write fails.
- **Meter Bridge**: CLIP and peak in the VU and history styles (with *peak hold* unified across the
  four views), scale ruler **sticky** while scrolling, remove per strip, bounded marquee,
  typography that scales with the zoom, aligned column header (before, each label dropped up to
  30 px to the right), a bank's own empty state and tabs with Win95 relief.
- **Win95 authenticity**: flat title bars (the gradient was Win98's), 1 px context menu with a hard
  shadow, `[!]`/`[>]` instead of emoji, native colour picker replaced by validated hex,
  status LED with no glow, and 19 dead `border-radius` removed.
- **Legibility**: the bridge's peak numbers were invisible in the dark theme (1.46:1), the dB scale
  measured 8 px at 2.7:1, the CLIP lamp when off could not be seen, the selected row painted
  nothing, and the Settings descriptions were at 3.6:1.
- **Real bugs fixed**: aliases were interpolated unescaped (they broke the markup; the single
  quote was missing too), an invalid channel list silently became channel 1,
  "saved" was announced when validation had rejected it, and an old macro armed **with no
  address** fired a real mute on channel 1.
- **Documentation**: re-audit report, `HANDOFF.md` up to date, the 14-09 port backlog
  updated (many boxes were already ticked), the 19-09 ledger closed and the invented build
  numbers of notes 0.2.23–0.2.27 corrected.

## The Dock icon on macOS 26

macOS 26 (Tahoe) forces app icons into the *squircle* shape and **composes a background** behind the
artwork; with the icon appearance set to Dark, that background is dark. It is not one of our PNGs: a
controlled experiment (a red disc over pure transparency) comes out with the same rounded square, and an
`Assets.car` from `actool` does **not** avoid it.

The only thing macOS 26 does not re-shape is a **custom icon**:

```
./scripts/set-custom-icon.sh "/Applications/WING CONTROLLER.app"     # after unlocking
./scripts/set-custom-icon.sh --check "/Applications/WING CONTROLLER.app"
```

Warnings, in order: take the app out of the DMG, run the **unlock** first (its `xattr -cr` deletes the
custom icon) and apply the icon **after** signing. Measured: with the custom icon,
3.9% of the opaque pixels are dark (only the marbling of the W); without it, 72.2% (the rounded square).
The full investigation, with seven screenshots, is in `scratch/icon-diagnosis-2026-09-21/`.
