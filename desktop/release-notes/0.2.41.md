# WING CONTROLLER Desktop v0.2.41

Build: 20260922.14 (macOS Apple Silicon)
Date: 2026-09-22

The multi-front audit round of 2026-09-22, with the fixes that came out of it. The full report —the 15
fronts, what is still open and the false positives discarded— is in `docs/MASTER_PLAN_2026-09-22.md`; here
is what changes for whoever operates.

## Show stop and live safety

- **A self-lock could hang the stream thread.** With the overlap policies "Queue one run" or "Restart",
  the engine emitted an event while holding the queue mutex, and that emitter takes the same mutex again
  (it is not reentrant): the thread hung, the meters froze with the window still on "Live", no macro
  fired and **PANIC STOP returned Ok without stopping anything**. The lock is released before emitting.
- **EMERGENCY STOP and "Disconnect / Stop" no longer fail silently**: if the backend rejects the command,
  there is a dialog, a log entry and a sound alert, and the local state is not touched (it does not lie
  saying "idle").
- **The "Send to the console" confirm** read four identifiers that do not exist and always showed
  `(no address) = (no value)` while authorising a real write: it now describes the selected step (mode,
  readable sentence and `address = value`) after resolving the target.
- **A dialog is in charge of the keyboard again**: `#win95-confirm` was not in the modal guard, so the
  keys 1-9 changed the macro behind it, and two overlapping confirms shared a node (a single OK resolved
  both). The guard now covers all backdrops and the confirm queues its requests. The legacy macro-delete
  dialog —which focused Cancel and cancelled with Enter— has been removed.
- **Saving no longer clears the "unsaved" warning before writing**, and the three "Export" buttons and the
  meter watchlist warn if they fail; "Connect" does not go ahead if the save it offered failed.
- The four long commands (`test_action_step`, `verify_macro_targets`, `usb_meter_stress_test`,
  `read_strip_models`) are `#[tauri::command(async)]`: they no longer block the window for up to 60 s.

## Protocol (Maillot 3.1.0)

- The **gate LED V2** is slot 6, not 8 (8 is `dyn gain`).
- The **`gate led` / `dyn state` states of `* V2`** return 0 or 1 and **are no longer divided by 256**: the
  `== 1` condition the editor offered was never met.
- The **FX scale** (`×6/2048`) applies to the band gain reduction, not to the four levels.
- The **user button** writes to the sheet `/$ctl/user/1/1/bu/mode` (string from the appendix), not to the
  `bu` node with an integer.
- A **matrix model** is read at `/mtx/...`, not at `/matrix/...`.
- A **datagram cut** in the middle of a group no longer decodes the following descriptor from a false
  boundary: the frame is cut and the complete events are kept.
- The **meters UDP buffer** is 32 KiB (the document gives 32k as the maximum packet size).
- **Documented ranges corrected** in the editor and in the preflight: high pass 20..2000 Hz, low pass
  50..20000 Hz, gate and compressor times (att 0..120, hld 0..200 / 1..200, rel 4..4000 ms), compressor
  gain −6..12 dB, knee 0..5 and FX mix 0..100 %.

## Instrumentation that told half the truth

- **`Dropped`** was a hard-coded 0: it now really counts the rejected meter datagrams (truncated or
  impossible to decode) and the row explains it.
- **`Rate` / `Bytes`** were averages over the whole session: they are now rates of the current window.
- **`Decode`** flickered to 0 twice per second (the periodic emitter sent a 0): it keeps the last measured
  time.
- **`Last RX` / `Last renew`** say "ms ago" instead of looking like latencies.
- The **health grid is marked as stale** when the stream drops, instead of staying frozen looking alive,
  and the OSC monitor badge recovers after a successful reconnection.

## Interface

- **"Only meters" hides the block's settings** (style/size/zoom, adding channels and banks) and leaves
  only the meters. **It no longer hides the rest of the window**: the button lives in the Meters card and
  only governs it.
- **Diagnostics: all the blocks collapse and, above all, open again.** Collapsing the OSC monitor hid the
  whole section —header and chevron included—, so it could only be collapsed once; **Probe** had no
  chevron. Now all four (OSC, Probe, Link & health, Log) collapse from their header or their chevron and
  the state is remembered.
- **Meter banks**: choosing a bank filters in both windows, the chosen bank **stays chosen** (before, if
  none of its meters was in the watchlist, the drop-down went back to "All meters" on its own and looked
  broken: now it explains why), "New bank" in the **Meter Bridge** asks which meters it stores —before it
  stored the whole watchlist, so the bank was identical to "All meters"— and a bank that stores the whole
  list says so too.
- **The macro row reads**: the pencil is **RENAME** and the `[!]` is **NOTICE**; **ENABLED** has its fill
  with a green gradient and a bevel facing out (before, flat green with a sunken shadow, which reads as
  pressed).
- **The "little square" of the colour tag** stops being a mystery: it is a button (with keyboard focus),
  the colour name is written next to it (`none`, `red`, …) and it has an entry in the Inspector.
- **The green Connection block is readable in both themes**: the "Live" chip in the dark theme was at
  1.6:1 (light grey on light green) and the green button, which is disabled during the session, still
  said "Connect + Listen": it now says "► Connected" and is greyed out keeping the text light.
- **No scrollbars in the working window** (page, columns, lists and readouts): the wheel and the trackpad
  still scroll, but they take up no width. Dialogs keep theirs.
- **Contrast and legibility**: the OSC path preview and the "Live" chip in the dark theme, the Meter
  Bridge scale and its rack in the light theme and the numeric scale of the graph in both themes (they
  were between 1.02:1 and 1.26:1); the dither pattern no longer covers the colour for HUD urgency, so
  panic is crimson again and not navy blue.
- **State that did not lie**: the Inspector no longer says "Live" permanently, the collapsed log counter
  follows the real one, the IP is locked while the session runs, the Connection button shows its state and
  saving with the draft armed no longer claims to have reapplied the library.
- **Layout**: Properties does not clip its close button, the HUD alert does not go off screen, "Compact"
  is really compact, the macro name is not capped at 175 px and the Inspector is not nailed at 165 px.
- The **packet counter** leaves the Live Meters header.
